Privacy Policy for Page Section AI Translator

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Page Section AI Translator is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how data is processed when you use the Chrome extension.

1. Data Collection and Processing

Page Section AI Translator does not collect, store, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to the developer or third-party tracking services.

  • API Keys: Your Google Gemini or OpenCode Zen API keys are stored solely on your local device using Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local). They are never transmitted to the extension developer or analytics servers.
  • Translation Content: When you select a section of a webpage to translate, the inner HTML/text content of that specific element is sent directly from your browser to your selected AI provider's official API endpoint (Google Gemini or OpenCode Zen) using your provided API key. No other tabs, browsing history, cookies, or unrelated content are accessed.
  • User Preferences & Saved Selectors: Saved section CSS selectors, target languages, auto-translate toggles, and per-domain custom instructions are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage.

2. Third-Party Services

The extension communicates directly with the AI service provider configured by the user in Settings:

  • Google Gemini API: Subject to Google Privacy Policy and Google AI Terms of Service.
  • OpenCode Zen API: Subject to OpenCode's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Ko-fi (Optional): Clicking the voluntary support link opens Ko-fi in a new tab subject to Ko-fi Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

All stored data (API keys, preferences, and saved domain sections) remains on your local machine until you remove saved sections via the extension Options page or uninstall the extension from Chrome.

4. Contact & Support

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our public repository: https://github.com/RhanCandia/page-section-ai-translator
